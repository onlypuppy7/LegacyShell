1. Spawn → Checkpoint 1

Walk across the ground from the spawn area to the green checkpoint at approximately `(5,1,2)`. This leg needs no jumping.

2. Checkpoint 1 → Checkpoint 2

Climb the two ramps beside checkpoint 1 and reach the flat upper platform near `(5.5,3,1.5)`. Avoid launching directly from the sloped lip.

Jump across the raised blocks in a zigzag:

`(7.5,3,0.5)` → `(9.5,3,2.5)` → `(10.5,3,0.5)` → `(12.5,3,1.5)` → checkpoint 2 near `(15.5,3,2.5)`.

3. Checkpoint 2 → Checkpoint 3

Move around the end of the central divider, then traverse the alternating blocks back toward checkpoint 3:

`(15.5,3,3.5)` → `(15.5,3,4.5)` → `(15.5,3,5.5)` → `(15.5,3,6.5)`

Then jump across:

`(13.8,2.6,6.8)` → `(12.8,2.6,5.8)` → `(11.2,2.6,6.8)` → `(9.8,2.6,5.8)` → `(8.8,2.6,6.8)` → checkpoint 3 near `(7.5,3,5.5)`.

The exact landing points can vary; the important feature is the alternating path around the divider.

4. Checkpoint 3 → Checkpoint 4

Jump through two consecutive block-and-overhead-block combinations. In each combination, the lower block is the landing surface—such as `(5,1,6)`—while another block directly above it—such as `(5,3,6)`—restricts headroom.

The overhead blocks can bump the player’s head during ascent, flattening the jump and reducing airtime. Each jump therefore needs controlled speed and timing so the player lands on the lower block without overshooting after the head collision.

After clearing both combinations, jump onto the ramp, climb it, and reach the yellow checkpoint 4.

5. Checkpoint 4 → Checkpoint 5

Enter the narrow lane containing the invisible barrier blocks, shown by the red collision wireframes. These barriers are solid and form the actual parkour route.

Jump onto the top of the first barrier, then continue jumping across the tops of the barriers in sequence. Stay centered enough to avoid sliding off their invisible edges. From the final barrier, jump to the far platform and reach the dark-blue checkpoint 5.

6. Checkpoint 5 → Goal

From checkpoint 5’s platform, jump outward across the gap toward the ladder on the goal structure. The ladder cannot be reached from the ground: it must be caught during the jump.

Aim so the player contacts the ladder while still airborne, transitions into climbing, and climbs onto the wooden roof. Once on top, walk onto the purple goal pad to finish.
